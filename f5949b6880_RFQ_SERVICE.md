**🔹 Summary of Project**

This is an RFQ for a Firm Fixed Price (FFP) Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract to provide comprehensive mowing services at the Greers Ferry Lake Project. The services primarily involve:

-   Inspection and policing of grounds.
-   Mowing and trimming of designated areas to specified heights.
-   Overall maintenance of the lake's recreational and operational areas.
-   The contract is structured with a base period and four option periods, aimed at ensuring satisfactory and timely performance in accordance with the Performance Work Statement (PWS).

**🔹 Acceptance Criteria**

*   **Performance Standards**:
    *   Mowing, trimming, and policing services must be performed to specified uniform heights (e.g., 1-4 inches for finish/bagged, 3-4 inches for park, 3-6 inches for dikes, 4-7 inches for bush-hogging) without skips, gaps, windrows, piled clippings, rutting, or scalping (PWS 5.3).
    *   Grounds policing requires all trash and man-made items to be in receptacles, and tree limbs disposed of at designated locations (PWS 5.2). A major defect is a combination of **5 pieces** of litter and/or limbs longer than three feet.
    *   Work site cleanup requires removal of grass clippings and debris from all sidewalks, curbs, paved roads, paved parking areas, concrete slabs, hardened campsites, and other structures (PWS 5.5). A major defect is evidence of **2 sidewalks** not cleaned and/or **2 individual areas** on paved roads, parking areas, concrete slabs, and other structures not cleaned.
    *   General performance of work must be in accordance with contract specifications, drawings, attachments, exhibits, and all applicable laws/regulations, according to generally accepted industry quality standards and practices (PWS 5.1.1).
    *   Services must be completed in a timely manner as per work orders and schedules, with satisfactory QC inspections and reports submitted (PWS 5.1.2).
    *   Safety and environmental compliance must meet all Federal, state, and local laws, and EM 385-1-1 and OSHA regulations (PWS 5.1.3). A major defect is evidence of a combination of **2 safety violations** and/or environmental violations or more.
*   **Inspection Processes**: The Government will inspect the contractor’s work within **24 hours** of the completion time specified in the contractor's schedule. Work completed on Friday may be inspected the following Monday (PWS 1.6.3.1). The primary method of surveillance is **100 Percent Inspection** (QASP III).
*   **Who Performs Acceptance**: The Contracting Officer's Representative (COR) and Quality Assurance Evaluators (QAE) perform inspections and determine acceptance.
*   **Rejection Consequences**:
    *   If work is not performed or performed unsatisfactorily, the contractor will be notified within one working day of inspection (PWS 1.6.3.1).
    *   Work must be re-performed or corrected within one working day of notice. Failure to do so results in deductions (PWS 1.6.3.1).
    *   An uncorrected major defect will result in a deduction in payment for that service (QASP Attachment C).
    *   Failure to perform satisfactorily in any critical element constitutes a major defect. Failure in any two non-critical elements in the same serviced area constitutes a major defect (QASP Section 8).
    *   Repeat offenses for littering/cleanup may lead to immediate contract termination (PWS 5.1.4, 5.5).
*   **Cure/Replacement Requirements**: Deficiencies must be corrected promptly. Work may be performed or re-performed within one working day of notice of nonperformance or unsatisfactory performance (PWS 1.6.3.1).
