2. The specifications must be listed, along with any specific brand names, part numbers, or NSN numbers. 

3. Quantities must be listed, not some vague "according to solicitation".

4. Delivery terms and place must be listed, not some vague "according to solicitation."

5, Delivery date must be listed.
