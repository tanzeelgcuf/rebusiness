### 🔹 **What They Want**

The U.S. Coast Guard requires comprehensive repair and overhaul services for aircraft components to restore them to an airworthy, Ready for Issue (RFI) state. All work must comply strictly with Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) technical specifications.

| Category | Main Tasks |
|---|---|
| **Inspection & Reporting** | - Upon receipt, inspect unserviceable components for missing parts and notify the Contracting Officer (KO) within 15 calendar days. <br> - Provide a detailed Teardown and Inspection Report (TIR) within 5 calendar days of inspection, detailing all parts requiring repair/replacement, including part numbers, unit prices, and labor estimates. |
| **Repair/Overhaul** | - Perform all repair or overhaul actions in accordance with the latest revision of the OEM Component Maintenance Manual (CMM), drawings, and service bulletins. <br> - This includes complete teardown, cleaning, replacing all consumables and degraded components, and removing all corrosion. <br> - All USCG-specific modifications must be retained. |
| **Cleaning & Finishing** | - Thoroughly clean all components to ensure they are free of hazardous dust, including hexavalent chromium, cadmium, and lead, per OSHA standards. |
| **Certification & Documentation** | - Provide a Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) Airworthiness Release Form 8130-3, EASA Form-1, or an authorized Certificate of Conformance (COC) with each repaired unit. <br> - Maintain and include a legible component tracking form detailing all work performed. |

### 🔹 **Key Compliance Points**
- **Quality System:** Contractor must be ISO 9000 compliant or have a quality system acceptable to the USCG.
- **Technical Adherence:** All work must strictly follow the latest OEM Component Maintenance Manuals (CMM), service bulletins, and drawings.
- **Hazardous Materials:** Repaired components must be cleaned of hazardous materials (hexavalent chromium, cadmium, lead) in accordance with OSHA standards 1910.1025, 1910.1026, and 1910.1027.
- **Packaging:** All packaging must adhere to Air Transport Association (ATA) specification 300. Electrostatic Discharge Sensitive (ESDS) components require compliance with ANSI/ESD S541 or MIL-STD-2073.
- **Shipping Terms:** FOB Destination or FOB Origin are acceptable. EXW, DAP, and FCA Incoterms are not permitted.
- **Labor Standards:** This is a service contract subject to the Service Contract Act and the specified Wage Determination.

### 🔹 **Key Requirements**

#### Certification & Capability
- Contractor must be the OEM (Hamilton Sundstrand, CAGE 73030), an OEM Authorized Repair Center, or a Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) Certified 145 Repair Station.
- Must provide proof of certification with the quote.
- Must perform at least 50% of the work at their own FAA-certified facility if not the OEM.

#### Technical Standards
- All repairs must use OEM-approved parts and procedures as defined in the latest version of the applicable Component Maintenance Manual (CMM). No alternatives will be considered for specified coating requirements.

#### Packaging & Labeling
- **Prohibited Materials:** Popcorn, shredded paper, Styrofoam, or any type of peanut packaging are forbidden.

- **External Labeling:** Must include NSN, Part Number, Serial Number, Nomenclature, Purchase Order Number, CLIN, and Vendor CAGE Code.
- **Documentation:** The packing list and Certificate of Conformance must be placed on the outside of each container.
