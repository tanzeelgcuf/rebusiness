## 4. Market Analysis

### 4.1. Market Overview

The market for temporary portable steel bridges is a specialized sector within the broader durable goods market (NAICS 423000). There are a limited number of manufacturers with the expertise and certifications required for government contracts. Our research identified three primary vendors:

*   **Acrow Corporation:** A well-established company with a strong reputation for quality and reliability. They have a proven track record of successful government projects.
*   **EnviroBridge:** A smaller company specializing in portable bridges for the forestry industry. They offer innovative designs but have less experience with federal contracts.
*   **Mast Equipment:** A supplier of various logging equipment, including steel bridges. They are a smaller player in the market and have limited manufacturing capacity.

## 6. Proposed Solution

We will provide one Acrow 700XS series modular steel bridge, which is a prefabricated, modular steel bridge system. The bridge will be 40 feet long and 12 feet wide, with a load capacity that exceeds the requirements for logging operations.

The bridge is designed for rapid assembly and disassembly, making it ideal for temporary use. It is also hot-dip galvanized to protect against corrosion, ensuring a long service life.
