🔹 Scope of Work
	•	Site Preparation: Activities including mowing, broadcast herbicide spray, discing, and burning to prepare sites for planting.
	•	Seeding and Planting: Establishment of three distinct planting zones: Bank Stabilization, Floodplain Forest, and Oak Savanna, using specified seed mixes, live stakes, and bare-root/container trees.
	•	Invasive Species Removal: Control of woody and herbaceous invasive species within designated forest preservation areas using mechanical and chemical methods.
	•	Maintenance and Monitoring: Three years of ongoing site maintenance (mowing, spot spraying, etc.) and annual vegetation monitoring with detailed reporting to ensure performance standards are met.
	•	Boulder Placement: Placement of approximately 30 boulders to prevent vehicular access at the entrance to Site 38b.
	•	Contractor must perform all or none of the listed items.
