	•	Seeding and Planting: Plant native trees, live stakes, and seed mixes across three distinct zones: Bank Stabilization (0.3 acres), Floodplain Forest (47.1 acres), and Oak Savanna (21.4 acres).

	•	Maintenance and Monitoring: Conduct 3 years of site maintenance (mowing, spraying, anti-herbivory measures) and annual vegetation monitoring to ensure performance standards are met. Submit annual monitoring reports.
