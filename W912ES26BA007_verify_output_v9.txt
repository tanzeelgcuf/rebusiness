🔹 Scope of Work
	•	Site Preparation: Prepare sites for planting via activities including mowing, broadcast spray, discing, and burning.
	•	Seeding and Planting: Plant native trees, grasses, and forbs across three distinct zones: Bank Stabilization, Floodplain Forest, and Oak Savanna, using live stakes, bare-root seedlings, container trees, and specified seed mixes.
	•	Invasive Species Removal: Control woody and herbaceous invasive species within designated forest preservation areas using a combination of mechanical and chemical methods.
	•	Maintenance and Monitoring: Perform ongoing site maintenance (mowing, spraying, etc.) and conduct annual vegetation monitoring to track progress towards performance standards, submitting annual reports.
	•	Boulder Placement: Place approximately 30 boulders across the entrance to Site 38b to prevent vehicular access.
	•	Contractor must perform all or none of the listed items.
