🔹Summary of Project
Title: Fargo-Moorhead Forest Planting
Type: 3-Year Service Contract (Firm Fixed Price)
Purpose: To plant, monitor, and maintain native forest habitat as environmental mitigation for impacts associated with the Fargo-Moorhead Metropolitan Area Flood Risk Management Project (FMM Project).
Location: Clay, MN, Cass, ND

Total Work Area:
- Base Contract: 165.4 
- Option: 10.4

🔹What They Want (Scope of Work)
| Category | Main Tasks |
|---|---|
| **Site Preparation** | Prepare 68.8 acres (Base) and 4.8 acres (Option) for planting via mowing, broadcast spray, discing, and burning. |
| **Seeding and Planting** | Planting in three distinct zones: Bank Stabilization, Floodplain Forest, and Oak Savanna, using specified seed mixes (e.g., BWSR 34-262) and tree stock from regional sources. |
| **Invasive Species Removal** | Control woody and herbaceous invasive species within 96.5 acres (Base) and 5.6 acres (Option) of forest preservation areas using mechanical and chemical means. |
| **Maintenance and Monitoring** | Conduct 3 years of maintenance activities including mowing, mulching, spot spraying, and anti-herbivory measures. Conduct annual vegetation monitoring and submit detailed reports. |
| **Boulder Placement** | Place approximately 30 boulders at the entrance to Site 38b to prevent vehicular access. |
