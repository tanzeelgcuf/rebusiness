## 🔹 SUMMARY OF PROJECT
• **Project Title:** Fargo-Moorhead Metropolitan (FMM) Area Flood Risk Management Forest Mitigation Planting
• **Type of Contract:** Forest Mitigation Planting and Maintenance Service
• **Purpose:** To serve as environmental mitigation for impacts associated with the Fargo-Moorhead Metropolitan Area Flood Risk Management Project (FMM Project) by planting, monitoring, and maintaining native forest habitat.
• **Location:** Cass County, North Dakota and Clay County, Minnesota
• **Total Work Area:** Base Contract: 165.4 acres. Optional Contract: 10.4 acres.
• **Project Objective:** To provide all labor, material, and equipment for a 3-year service contract to plant, monitor, and maintain native forest and prairie habitat across multiple sites, including site preparation, planting of trees and native grasses, comprehensive invasive species control, and meeting final performance standards by the end of the contract period.

## 🔹 WHAT THEY WANT (SCOPE OF WORK)
| Category | Main Tasks |
|---|---|
| Site Preparation | Prepare 68.8 acres (Base) and 4.8 acres (Option) for planting. Activities include mowing, broadcast herbicide spray, discing, and burning to eliminate competing vegetation. |
| Seeding and Planting | Plant trees, grasses, and forbs across three distinct zones: Bank Stabilization (0.3 acres), Floodplain Forest (47.1 acres base, 2.6 acres option), and Oak Savanna (21.4 acres base, 2.2 acres option). Specific seed mixes like BWSR Riparian S&W Seed Mix (34-262) and BWSR Woodland Edge South & West Seed Mix (36-211) are required. Planting densities range from 300 to 600+ trees per acre depending on the zone. |
| Invasive Species Removal | Control woody and herbaceous invasive species within 96.5 acres (Base) and 5.6 acres (Option) of existing forest preservation areas using mechanical and chemical (herbicide) methods. Follow-up treatments are required throughout the contract period. |
| Maintenance and Monitoring | Conduct maintenance activities at least twice per growing season, including mowing, mulching, spot spraying, and anti-herbivory measures. Perform annual vegetation monitoring to track progress against performance standards and submit comprehensive annual monitoring reports. |
| Boulder Placement | In Year 3, place approximately 30 boulders (one large, others approx. 2'x2'x2') across the entrance to Site 38b to prevent vehicular access. |

## 🔹 ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (END OF YEAR 3)
• Final acceptance is contingent on meeting all Year 3 Final Vegetation Performance Standards by the end of the contract period.
• Floodplain Forest Areas: Must have >450 seedlings/acre >30" tall and >300 seedlings/acre >42" tall.
• Oak Savanna Areas: Must have >300 seedlings/acre >30" tall and >250 seedlings/acre >42" tall, with >50% relative aerial cover of native forbs and grasses.
• All Areas: Must have <25 woody invasive stems (>12" tall) per acre.
• All Areas: Must have <15% relative aerial cover of herbaceous invasive species.
• All Areas: Must be free of noxious weeds as defined by ND and MN state laws.
• A final site walk-through will be conducted in Year 3 to identify and require correction of any deficiencies prior to acceptance.
