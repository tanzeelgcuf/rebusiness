🔹What They Want (Scope of Work)
| Category | Main Tasks |
|---|---|
| **Site Preparation** | Prepare sites for planting and establishment of Floodplain Forest, Bank Stabilization, and Oak Savanna areas. Activities may include mowing, broadcast spray, discing, and burning. |
| **Seeding and Planting** | Plant native trees, grasses, and forbs across three distinct zones: Bank Stabilization, Floodplain Forest, and Oak Savanna, using specified seed mixes, bare-root seedlings, container trees, and live stakes. |
| **Invasive Species Removal and Control** | Control woody and herbaceous invasive species within forest preservation areas and planting zones through mechanical or chemical means to meet performance standards. |
| **Maintenance and Monitoring** | Conduct annual maintenance (mowing, spraying, anti-herbivory measures) and vegetation monitoring for 3 years to ensure successful establishment and meet final performance standards. |
