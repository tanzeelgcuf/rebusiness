Bushings Manufacturers and Suppliers in the USA and Canada

Bushings is an umbrella term that covers several types of products. Bushings come in many types and varieties, but generally they are distinguished by either mechanical or electrical applications. Mechanical bushings are any machine parts that serve to reduce friction or part wear between moving parts such as rotating shafts, to space out or to separate machine components, or to serve as connecting adapters between parts of differing diameters. Mechanical bushings are types of bearings that are composed only of a single part, i.e. themselves. Electrical bushings are used primarily in electrical power transmission applications for allowing safe passage of electrical energy, and are are available in a variety of sizes depending on the application and power requirements; they are insulating devices or systems consisting of conductors surrounded by insulation that typically allow electrical energy to pass without arcing, burning, or otherwise leaking the energy to adjacent components or structures. Electrical bushings are commonly found on transformers and other high voltage applications.
