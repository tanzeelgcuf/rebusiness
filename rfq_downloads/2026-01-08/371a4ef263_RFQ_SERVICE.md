- Technical standards: The work shall conform to the most current applicable State, AFI, ETL, NFPA, and Unified Facility Criteria Standards.
- Specifications: Replacement equipment must be like-for-like in form, configuration, and functionality to the existing equipment: 2ea submersible non-clog pumps (XFP 4” EffeX, 208V, 3ph, 3.8HP), connected to 4” piping directed south through the lift station wet well wall, with flange connection elbows and adapters. The piping shall be replaced through the manhole walls out to the 6” gravity main connection, connecting at a 6”x4” wye. Existing conditions include a lift station manhole surface at approx. 974’ elevation, top of pumps at approx. 957’ elevation, and bottom of manhole at approx. 955’ elevation. The adjacent manhole contains two 4” gravity main pipes at approx. 967’ elevation, two 4” isolation valves, two 4” check valves, and a sump pump. One 4” line has a 2” hose nozzle connection with a cap.
