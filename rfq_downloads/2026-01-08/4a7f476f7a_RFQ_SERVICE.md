Roadway Pullout Work (Future TBD): re-striping and expanding parking for better traffic flow/RV access; adding a loop trail or boardwalk; thinning trees to improve views; implementing erosion control and revegetation; considering restroom options; updating signage at Oxbow Bend, Willow Flats, Snake River Overlook, and South Boundary. |
| Historic Building Rehabilitation | 4 Lazy F Historic Cabin Rehabilitation ($5–$10 million, 2027-2028): repairing and rehabilitating eleven log structures to support employee housing; foundation construction/repairs; sill log replacement; roofing; structural supports; upgrades to site utilities, fire suppression, and alarm systems; specialized labor for historic log building repairs; jacking/moving structures; sourcing logs; removing large cottonwood trees. Multiple Historic Building Rehabilitations ($5 million+, 2028-2030): String Lake Comfort Station Rehabilitation (2027) (restore 420 sq ft log restroom); Historic Beaver Creek Trails Office Rehabilitation (2028); Menors Ferry Homestead Rehabilitation (2029); converting Historic Beaver Creek Cabin #10 into staff housing (2030). All work must comply with the Secretary of the Interior’s Standards for historic property preservation. |
| Visitor Facilities | Mormon Row Visitor Contact Building (2027): deliver a new small, simple structure for Grand Teton Association operations, supporting interpretation and limited retail; open floor plan; utilization of construction assembly methods common in the Jackson area. Colter Bay Pedestrian Area (Future TBD): installing a small plaza at the T-intersection; adding accessible, ABA-compliant paths to connect key destinations (swim beach, amphitheater); incorporating firm surfaces, gentle slopes, rest areas, and clear directional signs; additional rest nodes; installing ABA-compliant curb cutouts in existing parking area. |
| Utility System Upgrades | Jenny Lake Lodge Septic System Replacement (Future TBD): replacement of aging lift station and septic system; new manhole at existing primary lift station site (to be demolished); gravity sewer line to new treatment location; new primary lift station with submersible pumps, above-grade valving/controls, secure weather-protected enclosure; treatment train of three concrete septic tanks; pressure dosing lift station feeding approximately 25,000 square feet of chambered infiltration system; demolition of certain existing system components. |
| Landscape/Site Improvements | Cattleman’s Parking Area Improvements (~2029): improving a short segment of existing gravel access road; constructing a small parking area with a vault toilet; decommissioning a portion of current road by ripping, topsoiling, and seeding to convert it into a hiking trail; new interpretive wayside. |
| Emergency Services Infrastructure | Lupine Meadows SAR Cache Rehabilitation (2027): removal of existing structure and foundation replacement; installation of a new prefab structure with utility connections in a highly constrained work zone. |
