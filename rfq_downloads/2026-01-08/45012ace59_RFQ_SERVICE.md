## 🏛️ Summary of Project

Title: INDIAN HEALTH SERVICES (IHS) IT SAAS MEDICAL EQUIPMENT PLANNING SOFTWARE
Type: Firm-fixed price purchase order
Purpose: The purpose of this Statement of Objectives is to purchase a software license for the planning of Medical Equipment and Furnishings, Fixtures, and Equipment (FF&E) for the construction of new health care facilities.
Location:
- IHS Office of Environmental Health and Engineering, 5600 Fishers Lane, Rockville, MD 20857

Total Work Area:
- Base Contract: Not specified in solicitation
- Option 1: Not specified in solicitation

Project Objective:
Currently, medical equipment planning and FF&E is done using spreadsheets, which has led to undesirable outcomes such as cost overruns, lack of awareness of changes, and version control issues. The objective is to switch from using spreadsheets to using a software as a service product to improve IHS operations and estimation for the planning and installation of medical equipment and FF&E.

## 🏛️ What They Want (Scope of Work)

| Category | Main Tasks |
|----------|------------|
| Software Features | The SaaS product shall be web based, have pre-approval for safe use with IHS Office of Technology, provide updated pricing at least quarterly, run pricing estimate reports to compare overall estimation fluctuations for projects, display in real time items that are discontinued, allow users to share access or grant no-cost access to other stakeholders to view the equipment plan and make suggestions, connect via an API to medical equipment & FF&E installation software for easy tracking, and have a REVIT plug-in to upload a REVIT file and extract planned medical equipment and FF&E for specific spaces. |
| Compliance & Standards | The software shall have access to the MILSTD 1682 for planning medical equipment to ensure IHS is following federal regulations and pricing when planning and purchasing medical equipment and FF&E for new health care facility projects. |
| Project Management | Projects shall be able to be archived and imported into new projects as needed. A master template list shall be developed and standardized for the beginning of all IHS projects to serve as a baseline for planners and project stakeholders to adjust accordingly. |
