Project Objective:
The contractor shall furnish all personnel, equipment, supervision, and materials necessary to design, fabricate, install, and commission a complete and functional Oil Water Separator (OWS) system that will be fully integrated into the existing facility infrastructure. Updating this facility to meet these standards will allow a more efficient and continuous way to test jets and their engines by reducing maintenance intervals between uses and reducing possible environmental hazards.

| Category | Main Tasks |
|----------|------------|
| Phase 1: Pre-Construction Planning & Submittals | - Submit design drawings, product data, and specifications for OWS system components (300 GPM, 99% removal of hydrocarbons >20 microns, gravitational and coalescing plate separation, meeting 40 CFR Part 112 & 122). - Submit specifications for integrated sensors, automation systems, coatings and structural materials (corrosion-resistant steel, 316L SS, FRP, epoxy-based coating), and control system schematics. - Ensure all products meet FAR Buy American compliance. - Submit a Work Plan including timeline, major milestones, roles and responsibilities, resumes of contract employees, and safety protocols (OSHA 30, HAZMAT). - Submit a Disinfection and Commissioning Plan detailing system flush-out methodology and testing strategies (hydrostatic, flow capacity, oil separation efficiency per EPA Method 1664 if applicable). All submittals due within 20 calendar days of Notice to Proceed. |
| Phase 2: Site Preparation | - Conduct a Geotechnical Survey to verify subgrade strength, drainage profile, and suitability for OWS weight and excavation impact. - Perform Excavation & Ground Stabilization, ensuring maximum water level is below required frost depth (32” for MHAFB), with a minimum of 48-inches clearance below frost line. Install compacted crushed stone base or reinforced slab based on geotechnical results. |
| Phase 3: System Installation | - Install corrosion-resistant tank system (316L SS or FRP) with internal epoxy coatings per hydrocarbon exposure specifications. - Integrate internal separation and retention system. - Install Hydraulic & Piping Network using Schedule 80 PVC/stainless piping with 0.5–1% gravity grade, designed to prevent degradation from petroleum-based products. - Install pre-filtration debris screen (<100 microns) and auto shut-off valves, including double-wall piping in critical zones. - Install Electrical & Automation Controls including Class I, Div 2 electrical enclosures for hazardous locations. - Implement remote monitoring, shutdown capability, level sensors, VOC monitoring, and maintenance alerts (for oil accumulation beyond 15% tank volume, hydrocarbon levels exceeding discharge limits, sludge overloading, and flammable vapor control). |
| Phase 4: Commissioning & Testing | - Conduct System Testing: 72-hour hydrostatic leak testing, 300 GPM flow capacity validation, and oil separation test per EPA Method 1664 (if applicable). - Ensure Environmental Compliance: Conduct NPDES compliance validation for effluent discharge quality, obtain final discharge permits and agency signoffs, and submit compliance certificate to base CE and contracting office. - Provide Training & Documentation: On-site comprehensive operator training on system operation, emergency shutdown, and routine maintenance. - Submit “As-Built” drawings and O&M manuals. - Provide a 1-year no-limit commercial warranty. |
