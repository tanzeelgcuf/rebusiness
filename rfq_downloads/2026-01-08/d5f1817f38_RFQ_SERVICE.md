## 🏛️ What They Want (Scope of Work)

| Category | Main Tasks |
|----------|------------|
| Multidisciplinary Design Services | Provide structural, civil site, relocations, electrical, mechanical, and architectural design services; including inspection, analysis, modeling, review, planning, preliminary studies, environmental and architectural services; general construction and engineer type surveys; field data collection; verification of existing field data; development of design build request for proposals (RFPs); and development of Plans and Specifications (P&S) with required calculations, quantities, cost estimates, schedules, and reports. |
| Specialized Engineering | Conduct bridge inspection, design, repair, and load ratings (including various bridge types and materials); perform utility inspection, design, and repair for electric, water, sanitary sewer, storm sewer, gas, and other systems; design and analyze masonry, reinforced concrete, steel, timber structures, deep/shallow foundations, piers, walls, cantilevers, slabs, columns, vertical construction, mechanical/electrical systems, civil/site features (grading, utilities, storm drain systems, sanitary systems, earthwork, roadways, bridges, cofferdams, parking lots, boat ramps, seeding, landscaping), relief well systems, and instrumentation. |
| Technical Support Services | Perform CADD work conforming to Tri-Service A/E/C CADD Standards and District CADD standards, using BIM (Inventor, Revit, Bentley BIM, Autodesk BIM) and traditional CADD (MicroStation DGN, AutoCAD DWG); provide state-of-the-art survey capabilities (ASCII format data, verification of existing data, right of entry coordination); conduct field and office investigations; provide engineering during construction support (registered staff, design staff advice/clarification, inspection); perform field reconnaissance to verify utility locations; design site improvements (grading, topsoiling, planting, seeding, walks, access drives, parking areas, fencing); and provide construction cost estimating using M-CACES M-II software. |
